About Text Statistics

Text Statistics analyzes text to provide comprehensive metrics including character count, word count, sentence count, paragraph count, reading time, and more.

Common Use Cases

  • Writers track word counts for articles, essays, and manuscripts
  • Students verify assignment word limits
  • Social media managers optimize posts for platform limits
  • SEO specialists analyze content length for search optimization

Tip: Paste or type your text into the input field. Statistics update automatically in real-time.
