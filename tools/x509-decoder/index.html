About X.509 Certificates

X.509 certificates are digital documents that bind a public key to an identity, forming the foundation of secure communication on the internet.

These certificates contain critical information: the domain name they protect, the organization that owns them, validity periods, cryptographic keys, and digital signatures from trusted Certificate Authorities (CAs).
