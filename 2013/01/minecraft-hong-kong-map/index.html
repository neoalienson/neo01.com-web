Minecraft Map for Hong Kong

1/23/2013
Misc
#Minecraft

First, create a 5000x5000 pixel height map, where each pixel corresponds to 1 meter in Minecraft. Considering that Hong Kong spans approximately 50,000 meters (50 km) from south to north, the resulting scale of the Minecraft map would be 1

While the mountainous terrain on Hong Kong Island is somewhat recognizable, the height map’s handling of flat areas isn’t ideal—it needs some smoothing.
